✦ how it works
01

join the community

join #3am in Slack.

come say hi, lurk silently or accidentally make friends. your choice!

02

build something

website, game, tool, app, whatever.

the only requirement? it has to be dark.

03

track your time

use Hackatime while you build.

yes, we need proof you actually made the thing and didn't just stare at VS Code for 6 hours.

04

submit your project

send it in before the deadline.

show us what you built, how long it took, and hopefully something that actually runs.

05

get your prize

pick your rewards.
