Requirements

Know the rules

Before you dive into the late-night build, make sure you check these boxes.

Age requirement
You must be 13–18 years old to participate. No exceptions.
Track your time
Log at least 1 hour of coding with Hackatime. It's how we verify your work.
Good standing
You must not be banned from Hackatime or a past Hack Club event.
Your own work
Submission must be your own creation. AI-assisted is fine, AI-generated isn't.
One submission
Only one submission per participant. Make it count.
Must be hosted
Your project must be live and accessible online. Dead links can't be judged.
Submission checklist

Before you submit

1
Join the Hack Club Slack — connect with thousands of teen hackers building things 24/7.
2
Verify with Hack Club Auth — confirm your identity and age eligibility.
3
Track time on Hackatime — install the extension and log at least 1 hour of coding.
4
Host your project online — GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify all work great.
5
Submit a working project — fill out the form with your live link and Hackatime screenshot.

Questions

Frequently asked

Everything you need to know before you start building.

Yes — YSWS 3AM is 100% free, like every Hack Club event. Hack Club is a 501(c)(3) non-profit, and your project is all we're asking for.
Any project you've worked on for at least 1 hour since the event started — websites, games, tools, weird experiments. If it's live online, it counts.
Anyone aged 13–18, anywhere in the world, can participate. No geography restrictions.
"3AM" is the vibe — late-night, focused, creative energy. Code any time during the event window, though we do encourage at least one genuine late-night session.
Anything — HTML/CSS/JS, Python, React, Svelte, Unity WebGL, Godot. It just needs to run in a browser and be publicly accessible.
After you submit, our team reviews your project and Hackatime logs. Eligible participants get a reward selection link via Slack DM within 1–2 weeks.
Solo submissions only — one per person. Get feedback from friends on Hack Club Slack, but the code you submit must be your own.
Tools like Copilot or a chat assistant are allowed as help. The idea, design, and architecture should be yours — don't just paste a prompt and submit.
